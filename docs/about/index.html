About / Technical leadership

Clear decisions.
Capable teams.

I help people grow, make sound technical decisions and build systems they can trust.

My focus is the connection between technical direction and the people who carry it forward. Architecture gives a team a direction; shared understanding gives people the confidence to act on it.

I start with purpose and constraints, make the options and trade-offs visible, and work towards a practical next step. That means giving equal attention to the quality of a decision, the capability of the team and the consequences of delivery.
