About / More than the work

A little
context.

I enjoy the point where someone stops needing an answer from me and starts finding their own.

My background is in software engineering, architecture and technical leadership. I still enjoy writing code. Over more than 20 years, the work has also become about helping people understand a problem well enough to take responsibility for it.

Some of my most useful lessons came from getting things wrong. I would rather explain where my reasoning failed than turn the result into a tidy success story.

Fatherhood has changed how I think about success outside work. That part of my life belongs here too, while the people in it keep their privacy.
