Delivery & reliability

A release with a way back.

A general delivery study that follows a change from preparation through verification, release and recovery decisions.

Status

Design study

Contribution

Authored study and release-review structure.

Prepare↓Verify↓Release ↶Always a way back.
The question

What would make the next change understandable, observable and reversible?

The approach
  1. Define the intended change, its boundary and the evidence that will show whether it behaves as expected.
  2. Establish a baseline, verify the candidate and describe the conditions for continuing or stopping.
  3. Plan recovery before release, including the limits of rollback and the checks needed after returning to the previous state.
The result

A reviewable sequence with explicit preparation, verification, release, observation and recovery steps.

Context & boundaries

A general design exercise. No real infrastructure, operational incident or client system is represented.
